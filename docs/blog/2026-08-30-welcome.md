# Introducing the Osmyka Knowledge Hub

Today, we are thrilled to officially launch the **Osmyka Knowledge Hub** — a dedicated engineering and documentation portal powered by Docusaurus.

<!--truncate-->

## Why We Built a Dedicated Documentation Hub

As our platforms — including the **AutoBook** 24/7 client booking engine and the **Osmyka AutoRepair CRM** — continue to expand across workshop deployments in Estonia and the EU, having a centralized, transparent technical knowledge base became essential.

This portal provides:
- **Comprehensive Product Manuals**: In-depth operational guides for garage owners, service advisors, and technicians.
- **Transparent Version Changelogs**: Continuous release notes documenting every feature, performance boost, and patch.
- **Architectural Specifications**: Detailed blueprints covering our 3-2-1 multi-level backup strategy, GDPR compliance, and edge CDN routing.
- **Developer API & Webhooks**: Reference specifications for third-party ERP, accounting, and SMS gateway integrations.

Explore the guides and let us know what topics you would like us to cover next!
