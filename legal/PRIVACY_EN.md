### 7. Data Retention Periods
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:
- **Consultation Inquiries:** Retained for up to 24 months following initial communication, or until the pre-contractual relationship concludes.
- **Contract & Project Data:** Retained for the duration of the active service agreement plus the statutory limitation period for civil claims (3 to 10 years).
- **Accounting & Tax Records:** Invoices, receipts, and payment proofs are retained for **7 years** pursuant to § 12 of the Estonian Accounting Act (*Raamatupidamise seadus*).
- **Server & Access Logs:** Retained for up to 12 months for security auditing and diagnostic purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

### 8. Your Data Subject Rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)
Under the GDPR, you have the following enforceable rights regarding your personal data:
1. **Right of Access (Art. 15):** Request confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy.
2. **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", Art. 17):** Request deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer legally required.
4. **Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18):** Request limitation of data processing under certain circumstances.
5. **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20):** Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
6. **Right to Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
7. **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw previously granted consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise your rights, please submit a written request to **info@osmyka.com**. We will respond within thirty (30) calendar days.
